Building the Future of the Concrete Industry, Together

Chico CIM Patrons is a nonprofit of concrete industry leaders: producers, contractors, suppliers, and alumni committed to the students of Chico State's Concrete Industry Management program. Through scholarships, internships, and mentorship, we help prepare the people who will lead our industry.

About the Foundation

Who We Are

Chico CIM Patrons is an independent nonprofit educational foundation. Our members are concrete producers, suppliers, contractors, consultants, associations, and alumni who share one goal: preparing the next leaders of our industry. Every Patron company has a seat on our board, and every dollar we raise supports CIM students at Chico State.

Why Become a Patron

Since Chico State joined the national CIM program in 2007, industry has stood behind its students. As a Chico CIM Patron, you will:

  1. 1

    Gain access to a pipeline of highly skilled, ACI-certified graduates

    Students immersed in business, science, and concrete-specific classes, with hands-on labs on high-tech equipment, ready for challenges in concrete technology, quality assurance, and management.

  2. 2

    Strengthen your company's reputation

    Align with a program supported by the CIM National Steering Committee, with access to exclusive events and networking, and a vote on the Patrons Board.

  3. 3

    Maximize your ROI through student success

    Paid internships with companies like yours, travel to industry competitions, and yearly scholarships that help students cover rent, tuition, and books.

About Steve Gonsalves

Steve Gonsalves is Chairman of The Conco Companies. A founding supporter of the Chico State CIM program, he pledged $1 million over 10 years to create the Steve Gonsalves Scholarship Fund. Steve believes in making an immediate difference in students' lives, supporting them now as they pursue their CIM degree.

The Program We Support

Where Concrete Meets Business

Concrete Industry Management at Chico State is an industry-driven degree designed to prepare students for the business of concrete. Students develop knowledge in concrete materials, production, and applications while building the business, management, and leadership skills they carry into the workplace.

CIM prepares students for the technical, logistical, managerial, and administrative sides of the industry—from classroom to boardroom.

Celebrating 30 Years of CIM

What began in 1996 with two students is now a national program built by the concrete industry. Chico State is one of five CIM universities, alongside Middle Tennessee State, NJIT, South Dakota State, and Texas State.
